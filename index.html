Let's be straight with you

AI tools are blocked on the school network and school devices. That's the rule, it's there for real reasons, and this site doesn't change it.

But we also know the truth: outside school, AI is on your phones, in your apps and all over your feeds — and pretending otherwise helps nobody. So instead of pretending, we made this. It's not homework and nobody's marking it. It's here so that when you do meet AI — at home, online, in your future job — you know exactly what it is, what it can do to you, and how to stay in charge of it.
